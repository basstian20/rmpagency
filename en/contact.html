04 / FAQ

Questions before contacting us.

Direct answers so you can begin without hesitation or a lengthy brief.

Can I consult first before starting?

Yes. The initial conversation helps us understand your needs and determine whether personal branding, consultation, or social media management is the most relevant step.

Do I need a complete brief?

No. Simply share your profession or business, goal, active accounts, and current challenge. We will help guide the next questions.

Can I start with personal branding only?

Yes. Personal Branding is Karya Raja’s main service and can begin without adding social media management.

Does Karya Raja work outside Medan?

Yes. Strategy, consultation, planning, and management can be handled remotely. Production needs will be adjusted to the location and scope.

What happens after I contact you on WhatsApp?

The team will review your context, ask any necessary questions, and guide the next step, such as a consultation, scope discussion, or proposal.
